# Company Discovery

## Overview
Sources and screens potential deal targets (platform investments or add-ons) against a defined investment thesis and screening criteria — size, sector, growth, margin profile, ownership structure — to build a prioritized target list.

## When to use this skill
- A deal team has a thesis (sector, sub-sector, business model) and needs a target list generated.
- A user wants to screen a broader universe of companies down to those that fit specific financial/strategic criteria.
- A user needs an add-on target list built for an existing platform company.

## Inputs
- Investment thesis: sector/sub-sector focus, business model characteristics sought (recurring revenue, asset-light, etc.).
- Screening criteria: size range (revenue/EBITDA), growth profile, margin profile, geography, ownership type (founder-owned, PE-backed, public).
- Any explicit exclusions (competitors already reviewed, sectors off-limits).

## Process
1. Translate the investment thesis into concrete, checkable screening criteria (size, growth, margin, business model, geography).
2. Identify candidate companies fitting the criteria from available data sources.
3. For each candidate, capture: business description, estimated size (revenue/EBITDA if available), ownership structure, and a brief fit rationale against the thesis.
4. Flag ownership/accessibility considerations — is this likely an active process, a founder-owned business not for sale, or a PE-backed asset nearing a typical hold-period exit.
5. Prioritize the list (e.g., high/medium/low fit or priority tiers) rather than presenting an unranked list.
6. Note gaps or limitations in available data for any candidate (e.g., private company with limited disclosure) rather than presenting estimates as confirmed figures.

## Output format
A prioritized target list: company name, brief description, estimated size, ownership status, thesis-fit rationale, and priority tier. Include a short note on how the screening criteria were applied.

## Notes / guardrails
- Be explicit about which data points are confirmed vs. estimated for private companies.
- Screening criteria should be stated explicitly so the resulting list is reproducible and defensible in an IC context.
