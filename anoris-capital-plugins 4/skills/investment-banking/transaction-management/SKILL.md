# Transaction Management

## Overview
Tracks the moving pieces of a live transaction (M&A, financing, capital raise): workstreams, open items, responsible parties, and timeline, so the deal team always has an up-to-date view of what's outstanding and what's next.

## When to use this skill
- A deal is in progress and the team needs a status tracker or checklist built/updated.
- A user wants to know what's outstanding before a milestone (signing, closing, launch).
- A user needs a timeline built or updated as the transaction progresses.

## Inputs
- Transaction type and stage (e.g., early-stage M&A process, live financing, post-signing to close).
- Workstreams involved (legal, diligence, financing, regulatory/antitrust, tax, disclosure) and their current status.
- Key dates: signing, expected close, regulatory deadlines, financing commitment expiries.
- Any documents or correspondence indicating open items (diligence request lists, legal markups, lender comments).

## Process
1. Identify all active workstreams for the transaction stage (e.g., pre-signing: diligence, negotiation, financing commitment; post-signing: closing conditions, regulatory approval, integration planning).
2. For each workstream, track: status (not started/in progress/complete), owner, and any blocking dependencies.
3. Build or update a checklist of specific open items within each workstream, each with an owner and target date.
4. Maintain a timeline view showing key milestones and dependencies between them (e.g., financing commitment must precede signing; regulatory clearance must precede closing).
5. Flag anything at risk of slipping — items with no recent movement relative to their target date, or dependencies that are behind schedule.
6. Summarize status in a format suitable for internal deal-team updates or client/counterparty status calls.

## Output format
A workstream-by-workstream status tracker (status, owner, open items, target dates) plus a timeline view of key milestones. Highlight at-risk items separately from on-track items.

## Notes / guardrails
- Keep the tracker current — flag stale items (no updates in a while) rather than letting them sit silently.
- This tool organizes and surfaces status; it does not substitute for legal or regulatory judgment on deal terms.
