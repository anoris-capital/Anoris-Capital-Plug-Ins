# Founder Outreach

## Overview
Drafts personalized outreach communications to founders/management of target companies and helps manage the outreach campaign (sequencing, follow-ups, response tracking) for a sourcing effort.

## When to use this skill
- A target list has been built (e.g., via the company-discovery skill) and initial outreach needs to be drafted.
- A user needs a follow-up message drafted after an initial touchpoint or introduction.
- A user is managing a multi-company outreach campaign and needs help tracking sequencing and response status.

## Inputs
- Target company/founder name and any available context (business description, why this firm/thesis is relevant to them).
- The firm's positioning/value proposition for outreach (what the firm brings beyond capital — sector expertise, operational support, network).
- Prior communication history, if any, to avoid duplicating or contradicting past outreach.
- Preferred channel and tone (cold email, warm introduction follow-up, conference follow-up).

## Process
1. Research enough about the target company/founder to make the outreach specific and credible — generic outreach performs poorly and reflects on the firm.
2. Draft a concise, specific message: why this firm, why now, why this company specifically (not a templated pitch).
3. Include a clear, low-friction ask (e.g., a short intro call) rather than an open-ended request.
4. For campaigns, sequence outreach with appropriate spacing and a defined number of follow-ups before pausing (avoid over-contacting).
5. Track response status per target (no response, responded, meeting scheduled, declined) so the team can prioritize live conversations.
6. Flag targets that have gone cold after the full follow-up sequence so they can be deprioritized or revisited later.

## Output format
Draft outreach message(s) tailored per target, plus a campaign tracker (target, outreach date(s), status, next action) for multi-target efforts.

## Notes / guardrails
- All outreach drafts require the user's review and explicit send approval — this skill drafts messages, it does not send them autonomously.
- Avoid generic, copy-paste-feeling outreach; specificity is the difference between a response and silence.
- Respect outreach cadence limits — repeated unsolicited contact after non-response can damage the firm's reputation.
